Site material calculator

Plan in, BOQ & cost out

Upload footing plans, floor plans, and roof plans — as many as you have, for as many floors as the building has. Tag each one, let the reader fill in what it can, then confirm the numbers and get a material requisition, an itemised Bill of Quantities, a steel summary, and a full cost estimate (materials, labour, overheads, profit, GST) for the whole building.

Steel / Bar Bending summary

Reinforcement by structural element, with wastage allowance for procurement.
Figures use standard Indian thumb-rule ratios and a 1.54 dry-volume factor for concrete, 1.27–1.33 for mortar. Treat this as a working estimate for procurement planning, not a substitute for a structural engineer's bill of quantities — get footing, column and steel sizing checked by an engineer before you order steel and concrete.
